## Description

Kaya of the Canopy is a retro action platformer in the spirit of the classic computer games of the early nineties: hand-drawn pixel art, tight controls and levels built to be learned.

Run, climb and swing your boomerang blade through five worlds, from the green Canopy Trail to the flooded Sunken Ruins, the sunlit Thermal Heights, the dark Termite Deeps and the volcanic Obsidian Nest.

SHAPE-SHIFT TO GET THROUGH
Transform pads change Kaya into a frog that leaps higher than anything else in the jungle, a fish that swims against the current, or a bird that glides across gaps no runner could cross. Every world asks you to use them in new ways.

FIVE BOSSES
Each world ends in a fight with its guardian: the Grove Warden, the Tide Maw, the Stormcrest, the Brood Queen and the Obsidian Heart. Every attack can be seen coming and dodged.

FEATURES
- 25 levels across 5 worlds, linked by an overworld map
- 4 forms: Kaya, frog, fish and bird
- 5 boss fights
- Keys, doors, switches, currents and updrafts
- Touch controls, or play with a game controller
- Game Center achievements for every world you clear
- No ads, no in-app purchases, no account, and no data collected
- Plays fully offline
